<h2>Case Study Methodology</h2>
<p>The subject of each case study is a biomarker which may be associated with one or more biomarker entities in the BiomarkerKB.</p>
<p>The biomarkers that are selected for these case studies are carefully chosen examples sometimes intended to challenge the conventional notion of a biomarker.</p>
<p>Biomarker entities are considered within the context of detecting specific diseases or conditions.  These case studies involve the discussion between three research scientists, one of whom has extensive, recent clinical experience.  Together, we conduct a literature review that seeks to address: 

  <ul>
    <li>which conditions/diseases are associated with the biomarker under consideration</li>
    <li>how the biomarker is mechanistically linked to each associated condition/disease, if known</li>
    <li>precisely which measured quantities constitue the biomarker</li>
    <li>which defined cutoffs or limits, if any, exist to differentiate normal measurement values from those associated with disease</li>
    <li>how the biomarker fits into the existing data model</li>
    <li>limitations of the biomarker, in the context of detecting a specific disease/condition</li>
  </ul>

<p>The panel of three researchers together create a short report summarizing our findings (with relevant citations).  We then format the report and upload it to this github directory.</p>
